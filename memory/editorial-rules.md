# Editorial Rules

## Audience

Write for three groups at the same time:

- people building AI products
- people tracking AI companies and capital markets
- people managing AI agents or digital employees

## Daily Article

- Lead with the strongest 1-3 trends before the raw news list.
- Every item must preserve at least one original source URL.
- Prefer official sources for launches, funding, model releases and policy changes.
- Mark signal level explicitly: red for high impact, yellow for important, white for low-priority information flow.
- Keep factual items concise; use critical judgment only where the source base is strong.

## Weekly Article

- Do not simply concatenate daily reports.
- Merge repeated events across the week.
- Promote patterns, not isolated updates.
- Each trend should answer: what changed, why it matters, who should care, what to watch next.
- Keep the weekly conclusion useful for next week's watchlist.

## Human Review

Human review is required before publishing when:

- a claim affects medical, legal, education, financial or safety decisions
- the article cites a single social-media source for a major claim
- the article names an individual in a sensitive context
- the generated article adds an interpretation not supported by the source material
